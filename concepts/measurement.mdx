## Definition

- A Measurement is a frozen eval definition over one Environment
- The contract: everything the loop does is scoped to a Measurement

## What gets frozen

- Splits, eval config, fingerprint at `monte init` time
- Why immutability matters: comparability across every run that follows

## Lifecycle

- Created by `monte init`, referenced by every other command
- Relationship to Runs, Lineages, and the Ledger
